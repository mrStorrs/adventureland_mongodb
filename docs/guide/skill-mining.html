Mining is a level 1–99 skill available to every character.

Equip a Mining pickaxe and enter the Tunnel. The Tunnel has three visible, account-private rocks for each ore tier. Click an available rock to target it; depleted rocks remain visible and show their respawn countdown. A successful attempt depletes that rock for 10 seconds for every character on your account, while other accounts remain independent.

The times shown use each ore's matching pickaxe. Any equipped Mining pickaxe can attempt any ore unlocked by your Mining level; the tool tier changes the attempt time and success chance but does not add another ore requirement.

Mining does not use MP or an ability cooldown. A failed roll gives no ore or XP and leaves the rock available. A successful roll gives one matching ore and Mining XP, with a possible Gem Fragment or nugget bonus. Ores stack to 9,999 and sell through normal merchant rules.

Mine Heathcliff stocks the Copper, Iron, Gold, Mithril, Adamantite, and Runite pickaxes, plus the level-99 Mining Cape. The cape costs 99,000,000 gold and adds five percentage points to Mining success.

For scripts, the browser action is use_ability("mining", rockId). Pass a stable rock ID such as copper-1 to target that rock, or omit the ID to select the nearest eligible available rock.
